Releasing new versions without downtime, while limiting the damage when something's wrong.

Feature flagscode ships dark; turn it on per user, percentage or storerelease ≠ deploy; instant kill switchflag debt; testing combinations
Shadow / dark launchmirror real traffic to the new version and discard its responsestest under real load safelyside effects must be disabled

Whatever the strategy: health checks gate traffic, you watch the golden signals (errors, latency, traffic, saturation) during a rollout, and rollback is automatic or one command away. For a POS in shops, also consider when you deploy: not during the evening rush.
