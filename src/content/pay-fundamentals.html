The mechanics of internet money: payment gateways, acquiring banks, card networks, interchange, PCI-DSS compliance, and settlement flows.

The 5-Party Payment Model

Customer ──► Merchant Store ──► Payment Gateway (Stripe/Razorpay) │ ▼ Acquiring Bank │ ▼ Card Network (Visa/Mastercard) │ ▼ Issuing Bank (Customer's Bank: approves/declines)
  • Interchange Fee: Paid to the card-issuing bank (typically 1.5% - 3% + fixed fee).
  • Chargeback: Customer disputes transaction with issuing bank. Merchant loses funds plus $15-30 penalty fee unless proven legitimate.
  • Settlement: Funds clear into merchant bank account on T+2 or T+7 schedule.
