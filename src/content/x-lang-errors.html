Traps when switching

  • Go: assigning an error to _ ignores it silently. Linters (errcheck) catch this.
  • Rust: .unwrap() in library code turns every error into a crash. Reserve it for tests and truly impossible cases (and prefer expect("why")).
  • JS: an un-awaited promise that rejects doesn't throw where you expect; it becomes an "unhandled rejection".
  • Python: a bare except: also swallows KeyboardInterrupt and your own bugs.
  • C: every function that can fail needs its result checked, including malloc, fclose and printf to a pipe.
