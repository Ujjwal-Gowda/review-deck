Practical rules

  • Lower the TTL (e.g. to 300 s) a day before migrating a server; the old TTL has to expire first.
  • A CNAME can't sit at the apex (freshon.in itself). Providers offer ALIAS/ANAME/flattening to get around this.
  • /etc/hosts overrides DNS locally: handy for testing a new server before switching DNS.
  • Plain DNS is unencrypted and spoofable. DoH/DoT encrypt it; DNSSEC signs it.
