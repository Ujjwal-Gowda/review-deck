  • Autoscaling GPUs is slow: nodes take minutes to start and models to load. Keep a warm minimum, scale on queue depth or GPU utilisation, and batch requests.
  • Cost maths: an H100 costs a few dollars per hour. Divide by real tokens per second at your batch size, and compare with API prices at your actual volume and utilisation. Self-hosting only wins when utilisation is high.
  • Keep an OpenAI-compatible API in front, so you can switch backends without changing app code.

With the GPU at $X/hour and T tokens/sec at your batch size: self-hosted cost per million tokens = X / (T × 3600) × 1e6. Break-even needs high, steady utilisation. Idle GPUs cost as much as busy ones.
